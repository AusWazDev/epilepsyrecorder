# What does the Change Register hold that the repo does not? — 26 September 2026 (Windows)

**The measurement that decided an instruction.** It changed the CURRENT DECISIONS block's rule
*"The Register governs on REASONING AND HISTORY"* (annotated that day, in both copies) and kept
the Register as a live record rather than an archive. Re-runnable by anyone who doubts it.

## The answer

Of a defined slice, **roughly 18% of the Register's content exists ONLY in the Register**
(6 of a 30-sentence sample, scaled to the unmatched pool: about 215 of 1,223 sentences). The
uncertainty is wide, **about 8% to 33%**, and **both ends give the same decision**: non-trivial
unique content, so the Register stays. The substantive part of it is research reasoning that
exists nowhere else. Sampling further was ruled out for that reason: a tighter number could not
change the decision.

**Found incidentally, and the reason the instruction changed:** in 2 of the 24 facts found in
the repo, the REPO held the NEWER reasoning and the Register the superseded version (samples 7
and 29 in `VERDICTS.md`). That is two cases, not a measured rate.

## ⛔ PASS 1 FAILED ITS DUPLICATE CONTROL, AND ITS NUMBER WAS THROWN AWAY

Pass 1 matched sentences by verbatim 8-word shingles. It reported **1,077 of 1,223 sentences
(88%) as Register-only** and 35 of 40 entries as REGISTER-ONLY: a spectacular number supporting
a spectacular conclusion, that the repo holds almost none of what the Register does.

**It was discarded because its duplicate control failed.** The apparatus control passed (a
sentence copied from `STATUS.md` scored 1.00; nonsense scored 0.00). But the 24 September
afternoon entry, whose facts are known to be in `STATUS.md`, was classified REGISTER-ONLY, 1 of
23 sentences matched. The Register restates in its own words, so a verbatim method measures
PHRASING, not FACTS, and can only over-report. **The apparatus was live; the question it asked
was the wrong one.** 1,077 is kept below as what it is, an upper bound.

⭐ **Recorded prominently because the tempting number is the one that survives in a summary.**
If this directory is ever cited for "88%", the citation is wrong.

## Pass 2: facts, by hand, on a fixed sample

30 sentences drawn from pass 1's 1,077 with `random.seed(26)`. Each was searched in the repo for
its underlying FACT in plausible repo wording, and every hit was read in context. Per-sentence
verdicts: `VERDICTS.md`.

**Both entry-level controls, showing the method can return BOTH answers:**
- **Duplicate control, PASSES:** the 24 September afternoon entry's facts are all in `STATUS.md`
  (`153 of 167`, `occurred_at_field`, "which one is newer", `30a561fa`), the entry pass 1 got
  wrong.
- **Unique control, PASSES:** the 24 September addendum's promise table is not in the repo.
  `STATUS.md` itself defers to the Register for it (*"Details in the Change Register entry"*),
  and its verdict *"UNDERSTATE the app"* returns 0 hits.

## Scope and definitions

- **Slice:** the 40 Register entries immediately before the heading beginning
  `## Tier A: a restore`, which were entries 40 to 79 of 81 on 26 September 2026 (30 August to
  24 September). The two entries written that day are excluded: they would measure themselves.
- **"The repo":** this repository's tracked text files plus all commit messages. **Not**
  `C:\dev\CLAUDE.md`, `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, ClickUp, or any other project.
- **Unit:** a sentence of at least 12 words after normalisation.
- **The Register is not in this repo.** It is
  `OneDrive\Projects\App Dev\Claude\Medical Event Recorder — Change Register.md`, on Windows.
  Its SHA-256 when measured: `df15ace04bfba3f2840cc55017a02f8db34673e04a0cd595a1b13cf8627f813a`.
  A later append does not move the slice; an edit to an earlier entry would.
